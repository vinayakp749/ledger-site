Ledger
B2B SaaS · Third-Party Risk Management

Vendor security review, grounded in evidence.

Ledger is a third-party risk and vendor security review platform designed to help organizations analyze security evidence, interpret requirements, map controls, and support structured trust decisions.

Evidence extraction

Turn security and policy documents into structured, citation-backed evidence for review.

Requirement interpretation

Generate structured requirement candidates and control-mapping proposals while preserving human authority.

Audit-ready workflow

Keep review decisions traceable with tenant isolation, human review, and evidence-linked audit trails.

AI-assisted, human-controlled

Ledger uses AI to assist with evidence extraction, policy interpretation, and control mapping. AI-generated outputs are validated by deterministic rules and reviewed by humans before final security or compliance decisions.
